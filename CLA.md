# Riddim Contributor Licence Agreement

**Status: a plain-language draft, version 0.1 (2026-11-21). It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.** It says what the project needs and why; the wording may be tightened, and contributors will be asked to agree again only if the substance changes.

## Why this exists

Riddim is published under the GNU AGPL, version 3 only, and its owner intends to offer it under a commercial licence as well, to people who cannot use AGPL code (see the vision, §6). That is only possible if the owner has the right to license every part of the code on other terms. A sign-off under the Developer Certificate of Origin does not give that right: it says where a contribution came from and that it may be published under the project's open licence, and nothing more. So contributions need a licence grant as well.

This is a **licence grant, not an assignment**. You keep the copyright in what you write, and you can use it anywhere else, on any terms you like.

## The parties

- **"The Owner"**: Kamino Research Corporation (legal entity: Bradgate Capital Management Pte Ltd, Singapore), the copyright owner of Riddim.
- **"You"**: the person or organisation making a contribution. If your employer has rights in what you write, "You" must be able to speak for those rights too: get your employer's agreement first, or have your employer agree to this document.
- **"Contribution"**: anything you intentionally submit to the project for inclusion: code, documentation, tests, data, in a pull request or by any other route.

2. **Patent licence.** You grant the Owner, and everyone who receives the Contribution through the project, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable licence under any patent claims you can license that are necessarily infringed by your Contribution alone or by its combination with the project as it stood when you submitted it.

## What the Owner promises

3. **It stays open.** Any Contribution the Owner accepts will be made available under the GNU AGPL version 3 (or, should the project's open licence ever change, under a licence approved by the Open Source Initiative), in addition to any other terms the Owner offers.

## What you confirm

4. The Contribution is your own original work, or you have the right to submit it under these terms, and you say so by signing off each commit under the Developer Certificate of Origin (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).
5. If any part of the Contribution is not your own work (code from elsewhere, text produced by a tool whose terms restrict its output, anything under another licence), you say which part and under what terms when you submit it.
6. You are not aware of any claim, licence or agreement that conflicts with these grants. If you later become aware of one, you will tell the Owner.
7. The Contribution is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind, except for what you confirm above.

## How to agree

State in your first pull request: *"I have read the Riddim Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA.md, version 0.1) and I agree to it."* That statement covers that pull request and your later Contributions, until a new version of this document asks for it again. An organisation agrees by having someone authorised to do so make the same statement on its behalf, naming the organisation.
